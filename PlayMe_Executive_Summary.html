Executive Summary • 2026

The Problem

The music streaming market is massive — and massively broken.

The global music streaming market is worth $54.1 billion (2025) and is projected to exceed $115 billion by 2030, growing at a CAGR of 17–21%. Yet the industry is riddled with problems that incumbents have no incentive to solve.

ProblemImpact
Algorithm FatigueUsers feel trapped in "echo chambers" — platforms prioritize safe, repetitive hits over genuine music discovery. 73% of Gen Z listeners report wanting more diverse recommendations.
Feature BloatSpotify, Apple Music, and others aggressively push podcasts, audiobooks, and video content — alienating music-first listeners who just want to hear songs.
Emerging Market FailureIn India, 96% of smartphone users stream music, but only 38% have ever paid. Current pricing models fail to convert the next billion users.
Artist ExploitationThe "pro-rata" royalty model funnels money to superstars. Mid-tier artists earn fractions of a penny per stream, making music unsustainable as a career.
Content Saturation150,000+ tracks uploaded daily — including floods of AI-generated spam — makes quality discovery nearly impossible.
💡 Core Insight
Users don't need another music library. They need a smarter, more intentional listening experience that feels premium, personal, and alive.

The Solution: PlayMe

A music-first streaming platform built for how people actually want to listen.

🎵

Immersive Cinematic Player

Full-screen dynamic backgrounds, adjustable YouTube video overlays (Full → Vivid → Ambient → Dim), and glassmorphic premium UI.

🎤

Word-by-Word Synced Lyrics

Real-time karaoke-style lyrics with adjustable timing offset — far beyond the line-by-line lyrics offered by competitors.

🤖

AI-Powered Intelligence

Gemini-powered Vibe Queues, mood detection, AI music tutor, and smart track summaries that understand context.

🎪

Live Concert Streaming

Browse and watch live performances, festivals, and concerts in real-time — a feature no competitor bundles natively.

Technology & Architecture

Built on a modern, scalable full-stack architecture.

Frontend

  • React 19 with Vite for blazing-fast builds
  • Tailwind CSS 4 for responsive UI
  • Motion/Framer for fluid animations
  • IndexedDB (Dexie) for offline-first caching
  • YouTube IFrame API for video playback

Backend

  • Node.js + Express REST API
  • JWT authentication + bcrypt encryption
  • Nodemailer for transactional emails
  • Google Gemini AI integration
  • YouTube Data API v3 + yt-search

External Integrations

  • YouTube — Audio/video streaming & search
  • Genius — Synced lyrics fetching
  • Last.fm — Music metadata enrichment
  • Spotify API — Recommendation engine
  • Google Gemini — AI mood & vibe analysis

Infrastructure

  • Currently deployed on Render
  • Ready for AWS/GCP migration at scale
  • PostgreSQL migration planned for production
  • CDN-ready static asset architecture
